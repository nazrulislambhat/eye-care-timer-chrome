🔭 Focus in the distance

Looking at a faraway object allows the ciliary muscles in your eyes to completely relax after close-up screen focus.

💧 Complete blinks

Screen use cuts blink frequency by 60%. Full, intentional blinks spread fresh moisture across your cornea.

📏 Arm's length distance

Position your monitor at arm's length (20–28 inches) with the top line of text level with your relaxed gaze.

💧 Hydrate & stretch

Take a sip of water, drop your shoulders away from your ears, and release neck tension while resting.

Frequent 20-second breaks reduce computer vision syndrome, digital dry eye, and end-of-day fatigue.
