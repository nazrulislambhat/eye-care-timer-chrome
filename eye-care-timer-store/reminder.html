Give your eyes a break

Look away from the screen for a moment.

20

Look at something 20 feet away

Look far away

Focus on something about 20 feet (6 m) away for 20 seconds, every 20 minutes.

Blink fully

We blink less while staring at screens. Blink slowly 10 times to refresh your eyes.

Check your setup

Keep the screen about an arm's length away, with the top at or just below eye level.

Move a little

Take a sip of water and roll your neck and shoulders while you wait.

Long stretches of screen time can lead to dry, tired eyes and headaches. Short, regular breaks help.
